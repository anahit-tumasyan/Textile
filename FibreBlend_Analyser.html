Being straight about it

What this is, and what it is not

The prototype is real and it runs. The models, the cleaning pipeline and the results above are genuine, and the predictions on this page are computed live from real measurements.

The fabrics are simulated. Their signatures are built from published laboratory measurements of real cotton and real polyester, so they behave realistically — but no physical scrap has been through this yet. Testing on real cloth is the next stage, and it needs samples and laboratory time rather than more software.

FibreBlend — cotton/polyester composition from near-infrared light. 601 measurements per reading, 1000–2200 nm.
